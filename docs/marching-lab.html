What this lab demonstrates

  • Every soldier has a stable identity-derived speed and lateral sample; no per-frame random noise is introduced.
  • All soldiers share the same step clock, while their individual phase reflects their stable speed sample.
  • Variation tightens by a factor of 0.8 after completed steps 2, 4, 6, and so on, down to a non-zero floor.
  • Changing the cohort size does not change the seed or behavior of an existing soldier identity.

Evidence boundary: this page is a self-contained browser visualization of the marching-cohesion rule. It does not load or execute the .NET Gestures assembly, Physics, ServerApp, a GPU crowd solver, or a 100,000-agent performance benchmark. For 100,000 logical soldiers, the browser draws a deterministic representative sample capped at 12,000 marks. The production C# contract remains MarchingCohesionProgression and MarchingCohesionState.

Next integration boundary: a host may optionally provide authoritative world observations or persist an experiment through ServerApp. The Gestures library and this Pages lab remain independently usable without that service.
